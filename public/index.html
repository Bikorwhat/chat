FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is chyatt really a 100% free chat app? ▾

Yes. chyatt is completely free with no hidden charges, paywalls, or limits. You can chat as much as you like, share files, and record voice notes at zero cost.

Do I need to sign up or create an account? ▾

No account, email, or phone number is ever needed. You choose a temporary display name each time you enter a room, maintaining complete anonymity.

How is my privacy protected during chats? ▾

chyatt operates with zero databases. All messages, images, videos, and voice notes live strictly in transient RAM while the space is active. Once all participants leave, everything is erased permanently.

Does chyatt work on mobile phones and older browsers? ▾

Yes. chyatt is optimized for maximum performance, minimal battery consumption, and smooth scrolling across all devices, including older Android phones, iPhones, tablets, and Chrome/Safari/Firefox browsers.
